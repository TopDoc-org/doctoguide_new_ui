What {{ appName }} is

An information and directory tool, built and operated by KnocDoc. Tell it what you are looking for and it helps you narrow down the kind of professional that could be relevant, then shows you listings nearby with hours and contact details.

It is a listings and information service, not a provider of any service itself, and not a booking agent. Whoever you decide to contact is your own choice, arranged directly with them. For adults 18 and over. Read the terms of use and privacy policy.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no charge, no subscription, and no credit card. You can start without creating an account.

Do I have to create an account?

No. An account is only needed if you want to keep your written summary and come back to it later.

Which languages does it understand?

Type the way you talk — English, हिन्दी, Hinglish, or another language — and it replies in the same language you used.

Does it book anything for me?

No. {{ appName }} is an information tool. It shows you the options and the contact details — whoever you decide to contact is your own choice, arranged directly with them.

What happens to what I type?

You are asked to agree before anything is processed, and you can read exactly what is kept and for how long in the privacy policy.
